I will join the Leibniz Institute for Astrophysics Potsdam (AIP), Germany, as a Humboldt Fellow in October 2026. I recently completed my postdoctoral appointment at Princeton University, where I worked from 2022 to 2026. I obtained my PhD in Astronomy from the Australian National University in 2022 and my BSc in Physics from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, in 2018.

I study how turbulence, magnetic fields, cooling and feedback shape gas in galaxies and galaxy clusters. Through theoretical modelling and high-resolution simulations, I investigate how cold clouds form and survive, how stars and black holes influence their surroundings, and how observations can reveal the underlying gas motions.

I use AthenaK, FLASH and PLUTO for my simulations. I also develop AthenaK-RM, my research fork of AthenaK, and AthenaResearchRM, a Python analysis package with CPU and GPU support.

Latest news

September 2026 · MHD mixing-layer paper submitted. We find that initially weak magnetic fields substantially suppress mixing and cooling in turbulent radiative mixing layers by reducing turbulent motions and interface folding. Cooling rates depend on magnetic-field orientation and remain unconverged at our highest resolution of 1024 × 20482 cells. Read the preprint →
